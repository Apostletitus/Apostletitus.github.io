About the Author

Apostle Titus Obinna Eze

Apostle Titus Obinna Eze is a Christian author and minister and the General Overseer of Universal Holiest Ministry. His writing is shaped by faith, spiritual reflection, vision and a desire to communicate messages of Christian life, sacrifice, purpose and transformation.

He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Religion and Education Management, with interests and experience in administration, leadership and entrepreneurial development.

Featured Book

THE ULTIMATE PRICE

2026 Edition

A Vision. A Revelation. A Commission.

THE ULTIMATE PRICE is a Christian work born from a journey of prayer, revelation and meditation. It explores the cost of a life surrendered to God and calls the reader to consider the seriousness of divine purpose, sacrifice and commitment.
